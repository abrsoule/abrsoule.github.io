Abrianna (Abbey) Soule

Plant chemical defense & plant–insect interactions across scales

I am a PhD candidate in Ecology and Evolutionary Biology at the University of Michigan, working with Marjorie Weber. My dissertation explores the ecology and evolution of plant chemical defense and plant–insect interactions across spatial, temporal, and trophic scales.

My work combines plant metabolomics, field experiments, and comparative approaches to ask how plant secondary metabolites shape — and are shaped by — the insects that interact with them.
